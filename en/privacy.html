Privacy Policy

Gomes de Mattos Sociedade de Advogados (CNPJ 64.824.149/0001-78) is the data controller for personal data processed through this website, under Brazilian Law No. 13,709/2018 (LGPD).

We collect only the data you enter in the contact form (name, email, organisation, subject and message). The form has no server of its own: on submission, it assembles your message and opens your own email application, ready for you to review and send. The website itself does not store or transmit this data; it only reaches us if and when you actually send the email, directly to our corporate inbox.

We use this data solely to respond to your enquiry, based on the preliminary steps towards a possible professional engagement (LGPD, art. 7, V) or, for academic and press invitations, on our legitimate interest in addressing them (art. 7, IX). It is retained for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and for the retention periods applicable to legal practice.

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. and uses Google Fonts loaded directly by the browser; this connects your browser to these companies’ servers abroad to display the page correctly, which may involve sharing your IP address with that technical infrastructure. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or audience analytics tools.

You may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, deletion or portability of your data, or information about its sharing, by contacting our Data Protection Officer, Karina Denari Gomes de Mattos, at kmattos@gdmadv.com.

This policy may be updated; the version in force is always the one published on this page. Last updated: October 2026.
